**Validation and benchmarks moved.** What this page held is now a section of its own, **[Benchmarks](/benchmarks/)**, with a
chart and a sortable table for each area. Its pages:

- [Benchmarks](/benchmarks/): the overview, with the headline numbers and a chart of the speed-up by area.
- [Speed](/benchmarks/speed/): the timings against every original tool, how they were measured, and the times on larger
  problems.
- [Accuracy](/benchmarks/accuracy/): how results are compared, the result for each tool, what is checked in other ways, the
  details by module and the [known differences](/benchmarks/accuracy/#known-differences).
- [The application's own tests](/benchmarks/application/): how the application is checked against the library, every
  comparison, the test counts per crate and the other verifications.
- [Reproduce](/benchmarks/reproduce/): the commands that repeat the tests and the timings.

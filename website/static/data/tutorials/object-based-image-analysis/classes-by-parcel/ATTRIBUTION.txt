Blocks of the tutorial 'Classes by parcel' (Terraflux, https://terraflux.org)

blocks.geojson: 33 city blocks of Lago Sul, Brasilia, Brazil, made by closing the streets of OpenStreetMap (highway = residential, tertiary, secondary, primary and their links) inside the Sentinel-2 clip of the series, with the data of 2026-11-22 (Overpass API). Attributes: block (1 to 33), faces (the kind of street along most of the block's boundary) and area_ha. EPSG:31983.
